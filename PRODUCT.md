## Users

Administrators of the Plano Aberto Filmes platform.

## Product Purpose

Manage content (articles, videos, news, partners), users, and moderation for the cinema portal. Monitor analytics and metrics.

## Positioning

A streamlined, modern administrative panel focused on quick publishing and clear analytics.

Desktop web browser, content creation workflow, dashboard monitoring.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Logo: Plano Aberto Filmes.
